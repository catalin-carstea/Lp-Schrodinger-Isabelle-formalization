H\"ormander Definition 7.1.1 and (7.1.3) define $\widehat f(\xi)=\int e^{-i\langle x,\xi\rangle}f(x)\,dx$ for $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$. Parseval (7.1.8) has factor $(2\pi)^{-n}$.
